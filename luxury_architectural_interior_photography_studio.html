The Transition

From Enterprise Technology to the Art of Spatial Curation.

My journey began in the high-stakes world of enterprise technology consulting—a field where absolute precision, meticulous attention to detail, and logical rigor dictated every successful outcome.

Driven by a lifelong passion for architecture, clean geometry, and visual harmony, I transitioned my analytical eye into the realm of Architectural & Interior Photography.

I don't just photograph rooms; I document the exact intent of the designer. By combining modern Flambient exposure blending (merging natural ambient glow with high-precision subtle flash) and perfect 3-way vertical alignment, I deliver publication-ready visual assets that capture true color, wood textures, and structural depth.
